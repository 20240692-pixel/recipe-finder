Creamy Chicken Pasta

Recipe ID: REC-101 • Course: Main Course

Ingredients

  • Fettuccine Pasta 250g
  • Chicken Breast (diced) 300g
  • Heavy Cream 1 cup
  • Grated Parmesan 1/2 cup
  • Minced Garlic 3 cloves
  • Olive Oil 2 tbsp

Preparation Instructions

Step 1: Boil the Pasta

Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook fettuccine pasta until al dente, drain, and set aside.

Step 2: Cook the Chicken

Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Sauté diced chicken until golden brown and cooked through.

Step 3: Make the Cream Sauce

Add garlic and heavy cream, simmer gently, then stir in Parmesan cheese until smooth.

Step 4: Combine & Serve

Toss pasta into the creamy sauce until coated and serve hot.

Dessert

Fluffy Pancakes

Recipe ID: REC-102 • Course: Dessert

Ingredients

  • All-Purpose Flour 1.5 cups
  • Baking Powder 3 tsp
  • Milk 1.25 cups
  • Egg 1 large
  • Melted Butter 3 tbsp
  • Sugar 2 tbsp

Preparation Instructions

Step 1: Mix Dry Ingredients

Whisk flour, baking powder, sugar, and salt together in a large bowl.

Step 2: Combine Wet Ingredients

In a separate bowl, whisk milk, egg, and melted butter.

Step 3: Batter Mix

Pour wet mixture into dry ingredients and stir until just combined.

Step 4: Cook

Pour batter onto a hot greased griddle, flip when bubbles form, and cook until golden.

Appetizer

Greek Salad

Recipe ID: REC-103 • Course: Appetizer

Ingredients

  • Cucumbers 2 chopped
  • Ripe Tomatoes 3 diced
  • Feta Cheese 100g block
  • Kalamata Olives 1/2 cup
  • Red Onion 1 small sliced
  • Olive Oil & Oregano To taste

Preparation Instructions

Step 1: Prep Vegetables

Chop cucumbers, tomatoes, and red onion into bite-sized pieces and place in a bowl.

Step 2: Add Accents

Add Kalamata olives and top with a block or crumbled pieces of feta cheese.

Step 3: Dress & Serve

Drizzle generously with extra virgin olive oil and sprinkle with dried oregano.

Dessert

Chocolate Cake

Recipe ID: REC-104 • Course: Dessert

Ingredients

  • All-Purpose Flour 2 cups
  • Cocoa Powder 3/4 cup
  • Granulated Sugar 2 cups
  • Eggs 2 large
  • Warm Milk 1 cup
  • Vegetable Oil 1/2 cup

Preparation Instructions

Step 1: Preheat Oven

Preheat oven to 180°C (350°F) and grease and flour two round baking pans.

Step 2: Mix Dry Ingredients

Whisk flour, cocoa powder, sugar, baking soda, and salt in a large bowl.

Step 3: Add Wet Ingredients

Add eggs, milk, and oil; beat on medium speed until smooth.

Step 4: Bake

Pour into pans and bake for 30 to 35 minutes until a toothpick inserted comes out clean.
