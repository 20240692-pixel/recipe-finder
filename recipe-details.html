Classic Creamy Tomato Soup

Recipe ID: REC-1024 • Course: Appetizer

Ingredients

  • Fresh Tomatoes 1 kg
  • Olive Oil 2 tbsp
  • Minced Garlic 4 cloves
  • Yellow Onion 1 medium
  • Vegetable Broth 500 ml
  • Heavy Cream 100 ml
  • Fresh Basil 10 leaves
  • Salt & Pepper To taste

Preparation Instructions

Step 1: Roast the Tomatoes

Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Halve tomatoes, toss with 1 tbsp olive oil, salt, and pepper. Roast on a baking sheet for 25 minutes until tender and charred.

Step 2: Sauté Aromatics

In a large pot, heat remaining olive oil over medium heat. Add chopped onions and garlic, cooking until soft and translucent (about 5 minutes).

Step 3: Simmer & Blend

Add roasted tomatoes and vegetable broth to the pot. Simmer for 10 minutes, blend until smooth using an immersion blender, then stir in heavy cream and fresh basil leaves.
